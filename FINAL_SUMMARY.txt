================================================================================
TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
================================================================================

DETECTION ALGORITHM:
  - Method: Background Subtraction
  - Preprocessing: Gaussian blur (21x21 kernel)
  - Filtering: Contour area threshold (>500 pixels)
  - Optimization: Process every 5th frame
  - Background learning rate: 0.05

ANALYTICS:
  - Footfall: Count of detections per zone
  - Dwell Time: Duration from first to last detection
  - Zone Efficiency: Visit rate = visits / duration
  - Hotspots: 70% threshold of maximum density
  - Heatmaps: Gaussian smoothing with σ=15

TECHNOLOGIES:
  - OpenCV: Video processing
  - NumPy: Numerical operations
  - Pandas: Data manipulation
  - Matplotlib/Seaborn: Visualizations
  - SciPy: Gaussian filtering

PERFORMANCE:
  - Processing: ~30 FPS
  - Analysis time: 1-2 min per camera
  - Total pipeline: 5-10 minutes
